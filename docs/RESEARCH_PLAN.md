# Research plan

This independent workspace provides reproducibility and evidence organization. The implemented equations are established baselines, not a revolutionary or novel RF method.

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow foundation | Validated records, calculations, app, reports and evidence bundles | Automated checks and Python/web parity | Implemented |
| Differentiated concept | Specific hypothesis and literature/program comparison | Independent novelty/nonduplication review | Not established |
| Experiment design | Medium, antennas, calibration, timing scope, controls and uncertainty | Reviewed versioned protocol | Planned |
| Channel validation | Measured loss/noise and residuals against the model | Raw data, repeated trials and uncertainty | No real data |
| Prototype evaluation | Matched baselines and application-relevant criteria | Controlled measurements and independent review | Not implemented |
| Program engagement | Applicable topic and scope review | Live notice/amendment and program-overlap review | No submission |

Next: choose a real scientific question and experimental medium, identify qualified collaborators, document antenna/receiver calibration and uncertainty, and obtain authorized laboratory evidence. Add propagation, waveform and transceiver models only with independently checked references and validation. No RF access or hardware is configured by this repository.
