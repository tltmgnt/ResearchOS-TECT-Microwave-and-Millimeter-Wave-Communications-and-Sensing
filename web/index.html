METHOD & CONTEXT

Useful calculations show their limits.

Idealized RF links

Free-space loss, declared fixed gains, extra loss and receiver kTB noise. Domain checks use the larger of wavelength and 2D²/λ as a screening boundary. Values outside it remain hypothetical and do not receive a threshold assessment.

Bandwidth and assumed delay

Ideal range resolution is c/(2B). Assumed monostatic range is cτ/2. Delay is quantized to the nearest declared sampling interval. These are not measured resolution, accuracy, detection probability or uncertainty.

Independent research workspace

These established equations support reproducible experiments, not a claim of a novel RF technique, validated prototype, spectrum authorization or DARPA affiliation. Review actual scientific evidence and applicable program requirements before making those claims.
